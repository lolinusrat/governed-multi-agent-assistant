> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY.** This document was written for a technical
> demonstration of the Governed Banking Policy Assistant. It describes a fictional
> institution, Meridian Retail Bank, and does not reproduce, summarise or represent
> the policy of any real bank. It must not be relied on for any operational purpose.

# 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how staff identify, contain and escalate suspected fraud
against a customer account.

1.2 It applies to all retail deposit, card and digital banking channels.

1.3 It does not cover internal staff misconduct, which is handled by People and
Conduct under a separate procedure.

# 2. Recognising a suspected fraud event

2.1 Treat an event as suspected fraud where any of the following is present:

- The customer states they did not authorise a transaction or payment.
- Credentials, one-time codes or card details may have been disclosed to a third party.
- Account contact details were changed shortly before an unexpected transaction.
- A payment pattern is inconsistent with the account's usual activity.

2.2 Suspicion is sufficient to act. Staff must not wait for proof before containing
the event under section 3.

# 3. Immediate containment steps

3.1 Where card details may be compromised, block the card immediately in the cards
system. A block is a protective, reversible control and does not require approval.

3.2 Advise the customer not to disclose one-time codes to anyone, including anyone
claiming to represent the bank.

3.3 Record a fraud case in the case management system within 30 minutes of the
customer's contact, including the time the customer first noticed the activity.

3.4 Where a payment has not yet settled, submit a recall request through the
payments desk in the same session.

# 4. Escalation tiers

4.1 **Tier 1 — Contact centre or branch.** Single account, exposure of 5,000 AUD or
less, no indication of an organised pattern. Handled locally with a fraud case
recorded.

4.2 **Tier 2 — Fraud Operations.** Exposure above 5,000 AUD, more than one account
affected, or the customer is flagged as vulnerable. Escalate within one hour.

4.3 **Tier 3 — Financial Crime Duty Officer.** Suspected organised fraud, suspected
money laundering, a possible internal party, or media or regulator interest.
Escalate immediately by phone and follow with a written case note.

# 5. Consequential actions requiring human approval

5.1 The following actions must not be completed by frontline staff acting alone.
Each requires review and approval by the named authority before the action is taken.

| Action | Approval authority |
|---|---|
| Unblocking a card blocked for suspected fraud | Fraud Operations Analyst |
| Releasing a hold on a suspicious payment or deposit | Fraud Operations Manager |
| Reimbursing a customer for a fraud loss | Fraud Operations Manager |
| Freezing or restricting an account beyond a card block | Fraud Operations Manager |
| Closing an account associated with a fraud case | Financial Crime Duty Officer |
| Contacting a third party or another institution about a case | Financial Crime Duty Officer |
| Submitting an external suspicious activity report | Financial Crime Duty Officer |

5.2 Containment is never blocked by an approval requirement. Where a protective step
under section 3 is available, take it first and seek approval for anything further.

# 6. What staff must not disclose

6.1 Staff must not tell a customer, or any third party, that a suspicious activity
report has been considered, prepared or submitted.

6.2 Staff must not share fraud detection rules, thresholds or model behaviour with a
customer.

# 7. Matters not covered by this policy

7.1 This policy does not state anything about the following, and staff must not
infer an answer from it:

- Whether a specific customer or transaction is fraudulent. That is a case decision,
  not a policy question.
- Criminal law, police process, or whether a customer should report a matter to police.
- The bank's insurance arrangements or provisioning for fraud losses.
- Scam-victim reimbursement entitlements under any external code or scheme.
- Detection thresholds and model logic, which are held by Financial Crime Risk and
  are out of scope for this document.

7.2 Where a question falls into 7.1, staff must not improvise an answer. Refer the
question to the policy owner named in this document.
