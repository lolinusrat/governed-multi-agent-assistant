> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY.** This document was written for a technical
> demonstration of the Governed Banking Policy Assistant. It describes a fictional
> institution, Meridian Retail Bank, and does not reproduce, summarise or represent
> the policy of any real bank. It must not be relied on for any operational purpose.

# 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how staff handle customer personal information held by
Meridian Retail Bank.

1.2 It applies to every channel and to every copy of customer information, including
notes, exports, screenshots and messages.

1.3 It does not replace the bank's obligations under privacy law. Where this policy
and the law differ, the law prevails and the Privacy Office must be consulted.

# 2. Handling principles

2.1 Access customer information only where there is a genuine business reason
connected to a task you are performing.

2.2 Collect only the information needed for the task at hand.

2.3 Verify identity before disclosing any account information, including confirming
whether an account exists.

2.4 Do not discuss customer information in public areas or on personal devices, and
do not copy it into unapproved tools.

# 3. Disclosure to the customer

3.1 A verified account holder may be told the information held on their own account
through approved channels.

3.2 A joint account holder may be told information about the joint account, but not
about the other holder's separate accounts.

3.3 An authorised representative may be told account information only where the
authority is recorded on the account and remains current.

# 4. Access requests

4.1 A customer request for a copy of their personal information is logged as a
privacy access request and acknowledged within five business days.

4.2 Standard access requests are fulfilled within 30 calendar days.

4.3 Staff must not assemble and send an information package directly. Fulfilment is
performed by the Privacy Office.

# 5. Consequential actions requiring human approval

5.1 The following actions must not be completed by frontline staff acting alone.
Each requires review and approval by the named authority before the action is taken.

| Action | Approval authority |
|---|---|
| Disclosing customer information to any third party | Privacy Office |
| Responding to a subpoena, warrant or regulator request | Legal Counsel |
| Releasing information about a deceased customer's account | Estates Team Leader |
| Adding or removing an authority to operate an account | Branch Manager |
| Deleting or de-identifying customer records | Privacy Office |
| Exporting customer data outside an approved system | Privacy Office |
| Disclosing information where a family violence flag is present | Customer Vulnerability Lead |

5.2 A request that appears to come from a law enforcement body, a court or a
regulator must be referred to Legal Counsel without a substantive response.

# 6. Suspected privacy incidents

6.1 Report a suspected privacy incident to the Privacy Office within one hour of
becoming aware of it, including information sent to the wrong recipient.

6.2 Do not attempt to recover or delete misdirected information yourself before the
incident is logged.

# 7. Matters not covered by this policy

7.1 This policy does not state anything about the following, and staff must not
infer an answer from it:

- Legal interpretation of privacy legislation or a customer's statutory rights.
- Whether a specific disclosure is lawful in a specific set of circumstances.
- Marketing consent mechanics and preference management, held by Marketing Operations.
- Employee personal information, held by People and Conduct.
- Data retention schedules for specific record types, held by Records Management.
- Cross-border data transfer arrangements and vendor contract terms.

7.2 Where a question falls into 7.1, staff must not improvise an answer. Refer the
question to the policy owner named in this document.
