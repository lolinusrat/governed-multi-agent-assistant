> **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY.** This document was written for a technical
> demonstration of the Governed Banking Policy Assistant. It describes a fictional
> institution, Meridian Retail Bank, and does not reproduce, summarise or represent
> the policy of any real bank. It must not be relied on for any operational purpose.

# 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets out how staff receive, record, resolve and escalate customer
complaints.

1.2 A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, staff
member or complaint-handling process, whether or not the customer uses the word
"complaint".

1.3 It applies to complaints received in any channel, including branch, phone,
post, email, social media and third-party referrals.

# 2. Recording a complaint

2.1 Record the complaint in the complaints system on the day it is received. This
applies even where the matter is resolved immediately.

2.2 Capture what the customer is dissatisfied about, what outcome they are seeking,
and any vulnerability indicators disclosed during the conversation.

2.3 Acknowledge the complaint to the customer within one business day.

2.4 Do not ask a customer to put a complaint in writing before it is recorded.

# 3. Resolution timeframes

3.1 Resolve straightforward complaints at first contact where possible.

3.2 Provide a written response within 30 calendar days for standard complaints.

3.3 Where a complaint cannot be resolved within 30 days, tell the customer before the
deadline, explain the reason for the delay, and give them the contact details of the
external dispute resolution scheme.

3.4 Complaints involving hardship or a vulnerable customer are responded to within
21 calendar days.

# 4. Escalation

4.1 Escalate to Customer Resolution where the customer rejects the proposed outcome,
the complaint alleges staff misconduct, or the matter has been raised more than twice.

4.2 Escalate immediately where the complaint involves a possible systemic issue
affecting multiple customers, or where regulator or media attention is likely.

# 5. Consequential actions requiring human approval

5.1 The following actions must not be completed by frontline staff acting alone.
Each requires review and approval by the named authority before the action is taken.

| Action | Approval authority |
|---|---|
| Goodwill payment or compensation above 200 AUD | Customer Resolution Team Leader |
| Any compensation above 2,000 AUD | Customer Resolution Manager |
| Waiving or refunding fees and interest above 200 AUD | Customer Resolution Team Leader |
| Correcting a credit reporting entry | Credit Reporting Team |
| Any written communication admitting bank liability | Legal Counsel |
| Closing a complaint the customer considers unresolved | Customer Resolution Team Leader |
| Agreeing a settlement conditional on confidentiality | Legal Counsel |

5.2 Compensation of 200 AUD or less may be applied at the point of contact and must
be recorded in the complaints system with the reason.

# 6. External dispute resolution

6.1 Tell the customer they may take the matter to the external dispute resolution
scheme at any time, and always when a final response is issued or the 30-day
timeframe is exceeded.

6.2 Do not discourage a customer from approaching the external scheme, and do not
suggest their complaint must be exhausted internally first.

# 7. Matters not covered by this policy

7.1 This policy does not state anything about the following, and staff must not
infer an answer from it:

- Whether a particular complaint should succeed on its merits.
- Legal advice on liability, negligence or a customer's prospects in a dispute.
- The external scheme's own rules, fees, jurisdiction or determination process.
- Employment consequences for a staff member named in a complaint.
- Hardship assessment criteria and financial difficulty arrangements, held by
  Collections and Hardship.
- Complaints about entities outside Meridian Retail Bank.

7.2 Where a question falls into 7.1, staff must not improvise an answer. Refer the
question to the policy owner named in this document.
